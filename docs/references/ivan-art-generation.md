# Иван: игровой спрайт варианта А

18 ноября 2026. Автор выбрал А на ivan-proportions-comparison.png и поручил реализовать в Unity.

Результат: Assets/Golovomka/Resources/Art/IvanOfficerWalk.png. Создан встроенным imagegen, без CLI/API fallback. Исходный лист 1254×1254, четыре ряда по четыре кадра, прозрачный фон. Ряды: вправо, влево, назад, вперёд. Столбцы: стояние, левая фаза, проход, правая фаза. Фактические границы ячеек округляются; непрозрачный силуэт обрезается и выравнивается по подошвам при загрузке.

## Запрос генерации
Use case stylized-concept. Generate ONE clean production game animation sprite sheet. Reference image is character design reference ONLY: use EXCLUSIVELY character А in LEFT COLUMN, compact big head short legs, not middle or right. Same blond 25 year old clean shaven male US police officer, dark navy peaked police cap with gold front badge, entire shirt and trousers dark navy, small gold badge on chest, black belt with silver buckle, black shoes. No gun in hands, no tactical gear. 2D top down RPG orthographic view, visible top of cap and shoulders, chunky crisp pixel art, limited palette. EXACTLY FOUR COLUMNS and FOUR ROWS on a square canvas. 16 frames TOTAL. Each frame occupies one equal square cell, 25% canvas width and height. Strict equal cell spacing no dividers no labels no numbers. Row 1 (top) officer facing SCREEN RIGHT, 4 walk phases; row 2 officer facing SCREEN LEFT, 4 walk phases; row 3 officer facing AWAY / SCREEN UP, back of cap, blond nape, back of uniform, no face or chest badge visible; row 4 officer facing TOWARD viewer / SCREEN DOWN, face and chest badge visible. In EVERY row column1 is standing idle with feet under hips, column2 is left leg stepping forward and opposite arm swinging, column3 is passing stance, column4 is right leg stepping forward and opposite arm swinging. Keep cap, head, body size and clothing entirely consistent across frames. Same design A proportions, about 3 heads tall. Each sprite centered horizontally in its cell with soles on the same baseline 90% down cell. Body including cap occupies 78% of cell HEIGHT and about 43% of cell WIDTH. All frames same scale and soles baseline; minimal walking bob. Leave at least 8% transparent padding on top and sides of every cell so no sprite touches adjacent cells. GENUINE transparent background everywhere around sprites; no floor, no chair, no furniture, no grey backing, no oval cast shadows. No scene, no border, no text. Crisp square pixel clusters, no smooth gradients or blur. Do not reproduce the comparison board; output ONLY the 4 by 4 sprite sheet.
