Transparent background around every object. All sprites fully within their cells with at least 32px transparent padding. No labels, lettering, grid lines, shadows outside sprites, no characters. TOP LEFT: wide brown wooden laminate desk, beige CRT computer on left, chunky beige keyboard, black corded telephone on right, small stack of papers, small brass desk lamp with warm light on far right. View overhead with visible FRONT FACE of drawers and desk, horizontal parallel edges, no isometric angle. Desk fills 440px width by 310px height, centered in cell. TOP RIGHT: tall grey steel filing cabinet four drawers with broad handles, front plus top surface visible, fills 210px width by 350px height centered in cell. BOTTOM LEFT: simple blue upholstered office chair with backrest and legs, fills 190px width by 240px height centered cell. BOTTOM RIGHT: wall mounted rectangular brown cork bulletin board with 4 chunky cream papers and colored pins, fills 360px width by 210px height centered cell. Pixel art aesthetic intermediate between 16 and 32 base tile art, chunky square pixels, limited colors, desk conceptual source size 72x48 pixels enlarged nearest neighbor. Absolutely no smooth gradients or fine texture/noise. Restrained brown, muted grey sage, blue upholstery, beige CRT, warm lamp accent. Maintained old functional police office: a few edge scuffs, mostly clean. Crisp silhouette, flat broad pixel clusters, same object detail density and same orthographic camera across all four cells. Items must have genuine transparent alpha surrounding them. No floor, no walls, no room background. One consistent sprite sheet.
